# Band Setup & Technical Requirements

## Stage Setup

### Ryan Kendig - Drums
**Position:** Center-back

**Equipment:**
- Full drum kit (kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, cymbals)
- Double kick pedal (optional, for faster punk beats)
- Heavy-duty hardware for aggressive playing
- Multiple crash cymbals for dynamic emphasis

**Technical Requirements:**
- Sturdy drum riser (recommended)
- Adequate stage depth for full kit
- Monitor mix with emphasis on bass and guitar

**Setup Time:** 20-30 minutes

---

### Bryan K - Guitar
**Position:** Stage right

**Equipment:**
- Electric guitar (high-output pickups)
- High-gain amplifier or amp head + cabinet
- Distortion/overdrive pedals
- Tuner pedal
- Cables and backup strings

**Technical Requirements:**
- Power outlet access
- DI box or amp mic (SM57 or equivalent)
- Monitor positioned for clear hearing of vocals and drums

**Setup Time:** 10-15 minutes

---

### Sallar - Lead Vocals
**Position:** Center-front

**Equipment:**
- Dynamic microphone (SM58 or similar)
- Microphone stand
- Wireless system (optional, for high-mobility performances)

**Technical Requirements:**
- Front-of-house monitors (or in-ear monitors)
- Clear sightlines to all band members
- Adequate stage space for movement and crowd interaction

**Setup Time:** 5-10 minutes

---

### Dan Gornstein - Bass
**Position:** Stage left

**Equipment:**
- Bass guitar (4 or 5 string)
- Bass amplifier or amp head + cabinet
- Cable and backup strings
- Tuner pedal
- Optional: distortion/overdrive for specific songs

**Technical Requirements:**
- Power outlet access
- DI box or amp mic
- Monitor with emphasis on drums for tight rhythm section

**Setup Time:** 10-15 minutes

## Sound Requirements

### Monitor Mix
- **Ryan (Drums):** Vocals, guitar, bass
- **Bryan (Guitar):** Vocals, drums, bass (moderate)
- **Dan (Bass):** Drums (heavy), vocals, guitar
- **Sallar (Vocals):** All instruments, with emphasis on drums for timing

### Front of House
- All instruments balanced
- Vocals clear and present
- Kick drum punchy and prominent
- Bass guitar present but not overpowering
- Guitar distortion controlled but aggressive

### PA Requirements
- Minimum 2kW system for small-to-medium venues
- Subwoofers recommended for proper low-end
- Stage monitors for each member
- Microphones: 1 vocal, drum mics, instrument DIs

## Load-In & Soundcheck

**Load-in Time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Setup Time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Soundcheck Time:** 30-45 minutes  
**Total Pre-Show Time:** 2-2.5 hours

## Performance Specifications

**Set Length Options:**
- Short set: 25-30 minutes
- Standard set: 40-50 minutes
- Extended set: 60-75 minutes

**Space Requirements:**
- Minimum stage size: 12' wide x 10' deep
- Preferred stage size: 16' wide x 12' deep
- Ceiling height: 8' minimum (for drummer)

**Power Requirements:**
- Multiple 120V outlets
- Total amperage: ~20-30A
- Surge protection recommended

## Notes

- All members arrive 2+ hours before show time
- Backup equipment available for critical components
- Flexible with technical limitations at smaller venues
- Can provide our own PA for intimate/DIY shows
