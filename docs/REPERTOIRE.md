## Arrangement Notes

### General Approach
- **Tempo:** Generally 1.5-2x the original speed
- **Key:** Often remains the same, occasionally transposed for better vocal range
- **Structure:** Simplified for punk efficiency, added breakdowns for live energy
- **Instrumentation:**
  - Guitar: Heavy distortion, power chords, palm muting
  - Bass: Root notes with occasional runs, consistent aggression
  - Drums: Fast beats, frequent crashes, powerful fills
  - Vocals: Raw, shouty, with strategic melodic moments

### Song Selection Criteria
1. Memorable melody that survives punk treatment
2. Lyrics that work with aggressive delivery
3. Song length suitable for punk format (1:30-4:30)
4. Crowd recognition factor
5. Ability to create mosh pit energy

---

## Musical Signatures

### Breakdown Sections
Most songs feature at least one breakdown section designed for:
- Mosh pit activation
- Crowd singalongs
- Dynamic contrast
- Live show energy peaks

### Tempo Changes
Select songs (notably "Sweet Victory" and "Goofy Goober Rock") feature tempo changes to:
- Build drama
- Create dynamics
- Maintain audience engagement
- Honor the original arrangements

### Call-and-Response
Key songs utilize call-and-response:
- "F.U.N. Song" (natural fit)
- SpongeBob Theme (audience participation)
- "Sweet Victory" (crowd chorus)
